How the planner works

Each criterion is summarized on an H3 hexagon grid in Earth Engine and ranked across the region. Planners weight the criteria to build a priority surface and connect recreation hubs and gateway communities with least-cost corridors. The planner then lists unprotected high-priority cells inside each corridor for easement or acquisition review.
