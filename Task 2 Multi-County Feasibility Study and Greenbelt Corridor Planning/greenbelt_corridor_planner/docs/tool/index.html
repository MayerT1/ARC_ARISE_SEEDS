Method

The region is tiled into H3 hexagons of about 5 km². Earth Engine summarizes land cover, terrain, canopy height, forest loss, satellite vegetation indices, rivers and protected areas for every cell.

The Geo-AI layer is a random forest that learns what strictly protected land looks like. High scores on unprotected land flag places that resemble what the region already protects.

Each criterion is ranked across the region from 0 to 1. Your weights combine them into a priority score.

Corridors are least-cost routes over the real trail, greenway, sidewalk and street network from OpenStreetMap and official trail layers. Walking an existing trail is cheap, a quiet street costs more, and building new trail costs the most. New trail is cheaper through high-priority or protected land. Routes therefore follow existing trails and only add new connectors where there is a gap.

The full method, data list and limits are in the fact sheet.
